FFXIV Disciples of the Hand · EXP planner

Collectable Leveling Ledger

Enter each crafting job's current level and current EXP in that level. The ledger takes it from there — for every job, it works out exactly how many of each Rarefied collectable to craft and turn in to level 100 (or an earlier target level, if you set one).

Below level 71, class quests (1-15) and Cosmic Exploration (15-70) are how you level. Cosmic Exploration needs Endwalker's MSQ finished first; if you haven't, Ishgardian Restoration covers 20-80 stretch instead. From 71 onward, Cosmic Exploration is still an option, but this tool models the collectable route. It assumes 100% quality (+300% bonus EXP) and no external buffs like rested EXP or food. "Craft my own components" prices out that collectable's full ingredient tree at NQ. All 8 jobs are simulated together in a handful of level windows, matching the batches FFXIV's crafting log unlocks recipes in (job level 70 gets you recipes up to level 75, 74 up to 80, 80 up to 85, 84 up to 90, 90 up to 95, 94 up to 100): EXP a job earns for other jobs while crafting its own components (priced using each job's level at the start of the window) is baked back into their level progress within that same window. Entering a world or data center pulls current market prices from Universalis (never the game client) to estimate gil cost for each job's turn-ins: buying every recipe ingredient as-is versus crafting all the way down to raw materials yourself. Each item is priced at its cheapest current NQ listing on that world/DC -- a snapshot, not a guarantee that listing is still there when you shop.

Each item name links straight to its FFXIV TeamCraft item page. "Copy TeamCraft import link" copies a Garland Tools-format list link with every item and its exact quantity already encoded -- paste it into TeamCraft under Lists → the import icon → "paste your link to the external list" to build a list.
